The first release of Tengen: a calm place to play and review Go on any server.

**Download** `Tengen-1.0.0-AppleSilicon.dmg` below, open it and drag Tengen to Applications. Tengen isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate, so the first time you open it, close the warning macOS shows, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway** next to Tengen. After that it opens normally. Tengen needs macOS 12 or later; reviews, estimates and playing KataGo need Apple Silicon.

### What's in 1.0

- **Play on OGS, KGS, Pandanet and Fox** from one app, with clocks that show byo-yomi periods, a soft tick in your last ten seconds, and automatic reconnecting. Fox support is experimental: Fox doesn't allow unofficial clients, so an account used with Tengen could be restricted.
- **Play KataGo offline** at a strength from 20k to 6d. It plays the way people of that rank do, using KataGo's human-style network.
- **Review any game** with KataGo on your Mac: your mistakes circled on the board, a graph you can drag through, KataGo's better lines, and **Try again** to find the better move yourself. Add a Claude API key for written lessons and maxims.
- **Your games**: online and KataGo games save themselves, marked once reviewed. Open and save SGF files, or load any OGS game by link.
- **A board worth looking at**: four woods, shell-and-slate stones, day and night rooms, zen mode, and click-twice-to-place for fast games.

### If something goes wrong

Choose **Play online**, then **Having trouble? Copy diagnostics**, and paste the result into a [new issue](https://github.com/rrivera1849/tengen/issues/new). Your names are replaced and passwords are never recorded.
